# サンプル写真

`cabinet.jpg`、`hallway.jpg`、`shelf.jpg` は内蔵画像生成ツールで作成した点検履歴用の素材です。

共通プロンプト

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. A single unstyled smartphone photo for a Japanese neighborhood disaster-preparedness app's sample inspection history. [Subject] Landscape 4:3, ordinary indoor daylight and realistic surfaces. No people, legible text, annotations, branding, damage, or watermark. One photograph, not a collage.

- `cabinet.jpg`: A full view of a tall brown freestanding bookcase and the adjacent wall in a modest Japanese apartment, with books on shelves and the top attachment area in frame.
- `hallway.jpg`: An apartment entryway with its closed door and the passage in front of it, with a single small cardboard parcel set against the wall beside the door, showing the floor and door together.
- `shelf.jpg`: A household storage rack, with two small lidded storage bins on a higher shelf and lighter objects below, framed so that the shelf levels and the items are clear.

`stairs.jpg` は住民投稿例に使用した [Stairs and handrails](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stairs_and_handrails.jpg)（Colin McLaughlin、CC0）を縮小したものです。
